Full System Overview

This causal loop diagram models how financial pressure, social support, stigma, and healthcare access interact to shape help-seeking and psychological distress among young adults in Kenya.

Loop Type Multi-loop
Polarity Signal Mixed
  • Financial strain increases distress and can reduce coping capacity over time.
  • Stigma and fear of blame can delay help-seeking, worsening psychological symptoms.
  • Social support and healthcare access act as protective balancing forces.
