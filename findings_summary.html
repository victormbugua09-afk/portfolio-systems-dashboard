Project summary

Stress, help-seeking, and mental health among young adults in Kenya

This project explores how financial strain, social support, stigma, healthcare access, and environmental pressures relate to psychological distress and help-seeking among young adults aged 20 to 29 in Kenya.

Key findings

Finding 1

Financial strain appears to be a major driver of distress

Across the synthetic sample, many respondents reported moderate or high financial strain. This pattern aligns with higher psychological distress and lower coping capacity.

Finding 2

Social support appears protective

Respondents with stronger social support and parental support generally showed better psychological outcomes and were more likely to seek help earlier.

Finding 3

Stigma and fear of blame reduce help-seeking

In the sample, stigma and blame were associated with delayed help-seeking, suggesting that social barriers influence whether people access support.

Finding 4

Healthcare access matters for recovery pathways

Poor healthcare access was linked to higher distress and lower help-seeking, reinforcing the importance of affordability, trust, and availability of services.

Systems thinking interpretation

  • Financial strain increases psychological distress.
  • Higher distress weakens coping capacity and may reduce sleep quality.
  • Low support increases the likelihood of stigma and delayed help-seeking.
  • Weak healthcare access and structural inequalities continue the cycle.

Implications

Policy

Target the system, not only the individual

Interventions are most effective when they improve access to support, reduce stigma, and strengthen youth-friendly service systems.

Practice

Invest in social protection and family support

Stronger social support and parental support appear to be protective and may be central to earlier help-seeking and improved wellbeing.

Research

Use the CLD as a discussion tool

The causal loop model can help stakeholders see how distress, help-seeking, and social barriers reinforce one another across time.

Portfolio value

Demonstrate analytical and systems-thinking maturity

This project shows a strong link between data handling, causal interpretation, and practical social impact storytelling.
