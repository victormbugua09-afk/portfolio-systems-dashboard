Health Policy • Data Analytics

Community Wellness Risk Index (CWRI)

The Community Wellness Risk Index was developed to improve how health-related risks are understood and prioritized across communities. The project started with a clear problem: health stakeholders needed a more structured, data-informed way to identify risk patterns and respond to them effectively.

  • Problem: Limited visibility into regional wellness risks and inconsistent access to usable health data.
  • Technical pivot: I worked on data cleaning and dashboard preparation across 45 regions using Looker Studio to organize and present the data more clearly.
  • Key results: The project improved visibility of regional differences, strengthened presentation quality, and created a stronger foundation for evidence-driven communication and program planning.
Outcome

A more interpretable and actionable view of community wellness risk, designed to support strategic health program decisions and clearer analytical storytelling.
